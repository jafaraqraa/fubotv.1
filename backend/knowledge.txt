2.1 Bundle Rules
The Home Energy Bundle combines one Atlas Home 5K Battery, one SmartGrid 6 Inverter, one Atlas Edge
Monitor, and standard installation. Its standard bundle price is 27,900 ILS before VAT.
The Home Energy Bundle receives a 5% promotional discount when the pre-discount order value is at
least 25,000 ILS and payment is made by bank transfer within 5 business days of quotation. The discount
applies to the bundle subtotal, not to VAT or optional civil works.
The Atlas Home 3K Battery is not eligible for the 5% bundle discount when purchased alone. Dealers may
have separate commercial pricing, but dealer discounts are not described in this handbook.

and one customer handover session. Additional cable is billed at 35 ILS per meter. Concrete bases,

4. Warranty & Returns
4.1 Hardware Warranty
Battery warranties cover manufacturing defects under normal use. They do not cover physical damage,
unauthorized modification, flooding, fire caused by external sources, incorrect installation by non-certified
parties, or operation outside documented environmental limits.
A warranty claim requires the product serial number, purchase invoice, installation record, fault description,
and diagnostic logs when available.
4.2 Dead-on-Arrival (DOA)
Hardware that is non-functional at first installation may qualify as DOA if reported within 7 calendar days
of delivery. AtlasTech may replace the unit after remote or onsite verification. Cosmetic damage alone
does not qualify as DOA unless it affects safe use.
4.3 Standard Returns
Unopened standard-stock hardware may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery with the original
invoice. A 10% restocking fee applies. Opened batteries and custom-order items are not returnable unless
defective.
Software setup fees are non-refundable once implementation work has started. Monthly subscriptions can
be cancelled before the next billing cycle but already-paid monthly fees are not prorated.
4.4 Approval Limits

Refunds related to fraud allegations, bank-account changes, or legal disputes must be escalated to Finance
and the COO regardless of amount.

5. Sales, Discounts & Payment Terms
5.1 Standard Payment Terms
Retail hardware sales require 50% upon order confirmation and 50% before delivery unless Finance
approves different terms. Automation projects require 40% on signing, 40% after acceptance testing, and
20% at production launch.
Monthly subscriptions are invoiced in advance. Enterprise customers approved for credit may receive Net
30 terms.
5.2 Discount Authority
Role Maximum Standard Discount
Sales Representative 3%
Sales Manager 7%
COO 12%
CEO More than 12%
Published promotional discounts can be applied without additional approval when all campaign conditions
are met. Discounts must not be stacked unless the campaign explicitly allows stacking.
The Home Energy Bundle's 5% bank-transfer promotion is a published promotion and therefore does not
consume the salesperson's normal 3% discretionary discount authority. However, it cannot be combined
with another discretionary discount.
5.3 Quotation Validity
Standard quotations remain valid for 10 calendar days. Solar and battery quotations may be limited to 5
calendar days when marked 'price-protected for 5 days' because supplier and freight costs can change.

6. Customer Support & Incident Handling
6.1 Severity Levels
Severity Definition Example
S1 - Critical Complete outage of a business-critical system with no workaround Production ordering system unavailable
S2 - High Major degradation affecting many users; workaround exists CRM sync delayed for all sales staff
S3 - Normal Limited impact or single-user issue One employee cannot access a dashboard
S4 - Request Information, configuration, or cosmetic change Change report column order
6.2 Escalation
S1 incidents must be assigned to the on-call technical lead immediately. If an S1 incident is not
acknowledged within 15 minutes, it must be escalated to the Operations Manager. If customer data
exposure is suspected, the Security Lead must also be notified.
S2 incidents should be escalated when no meaningful progress has been made within 2 business hours.
S3 issues are handled through the normal support queue.
6.3 Password and Account Requests
Support staff must never ask a customer to send passwords, one-time codes, private keys, or full
payment-card details by chat or email. Identity verification is required before changing an account email,
phone number, or billing contact.

7. Employee Policies
7.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 18 paid annual-leave days per completed year of service. Employees with five
or more completed years receive 21 days. Leave should normally be requested at least 5 working days in
advance.
Up to 5 unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used
by 31 March or they expire unless HR approves an exception in writing.
7.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive up to 14 paid sick-leave days per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave
lasts 3 consecutive working days or more.
Sick leave is separate from annual leave and should not be deducted from the employee's annual-leave
balance.
7.3 Remote Work
Eligible office employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Field
technicians and warehouse staff are generally not eligible because their roles require onsite work.
Remote work from outside the country for more than 5 consecutive working days requires HR and
department-head approval.
7.4 Probation
The standard probation period is 3 months. It may be extended once, up to a total probation period of 6
months, with written HR approval.

8.2 Receiving
Warehouse staff must verify purchase order number, SKU, quantity, serial numbers where applicable, and
visible shipping damage. Batteries and inverters must be serial-tracked before they are made available for
sale.
8.3 Transfers
Transfers between Ramallah and Nablus require a transfer document showing source, destination, item,
quantity, dispatcher, receiver, and timestamps. Stock remains in-transit until the receiving location confirms
receipt.

9.2 Retention
Standard support tickets are retained for 24 months after closure. Signed customer contracts are retained
for 7 years after termination. Routine application logs are retained for 90 days unless a security
investigation or legal hold requires longer retention.
9.3 Security Incidents
Suspected credential theft, unauthorized access, malware, customer-data exposure, or loss of a company
device must be reported immediately to the Security Lead. Employees should preserve evidence and must
not delete logs or attempt unauthorized forensic actions.
